About Beyond Basics

Opportunity should not stop at the basics.

We are developing a practical learning initiative to make English communication and digital literacy more accessible to school support staff.

Our purpose

Practical learning. Real-world confidence.

Everyday communication and digital tasks are part of modern life. Access to structured learning, however, is not always equal. Beyond Basics aims to create an approachable way for school support staff to practise useful skills and develop confidence on their own terms.

Our principles

How we put our mission into practice.

Respect first

Use inclusive language and treat each learner as an active participant.

Practical by design

Prioritise skills learners can practise and apply in everyday situations.

Improve with evidence

Use feedback and observed learning to refine content and delivery.

Privacy

Privacy by design.

Student accounts are handled by Supabase Authentication. When the progress database is configured, completed lesson IDs are stored in Supabase and protected by row-level security so each student can access only their own progress. The Student Suggestions and general contact forms send the information you submit to Formspree for delivery.

Only provide information needed to use the site or receive a response. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal details, or confidential information in a suggestion or enquiry. The project owner should review applicable school and legal requirements and publish a complete privacy notice covering retention and deletion before broad use with learners.

Accessibility

Designed to be approachable.

The interface aims to support keyboard navigation, readable text, responsive layouts, visible focus states, and reduced motion preferences. A full accessibility review with assistive technologies and representative users is still needed before claiming conformance to an accessibility standard.
