About Beyond Basics

Opportunity should not stop at the basics.

We are developing a practical learning initiative to make English communication and digital literacy more accessible to school support staff.

Our purpose

Learning with dignity at its centre.

Everyday communication and digital tasks are part of modern life. Access to structured learning, however, is not always equal. Beyond Basics aims to create an approachable way for school support staff to practise useful skills and develop confidence on their own terms.

Our approach is grounded in respect. Learners bring their own knowledge, experience, and strengths; the programme is designed to build on them rather than define people by what they have not yet learned.

Our principles

How we intend to work.

Respect first

Use inclusive language and treat each learner as an active participant.

Practical by design

Prioritise skills learners can practise and apply in everyday situations.

Improve with evidence

Use feedback and observed learning to refine content and delivery.

Current stage: Beyond Basics is being planned and developed. Programme details, delivery arrangements, and partnerships will be updated as they are confirmed.

Our approach

Designed around people, not platforms.

The technology should make learning easier to access—not become another barrier.

◎

Start with learner needs

Identify current experience, practical goals, and preferred pace before shaping learning activities.

▤

Keep it practical

Use clear instructions, familiar examples, guided practice, and short checks for understanding.

↻

Review and improve

Use feedback and observed progress to update lessons and improve accessibility.

Proposed delivery model

The initial planning model considers 30-minute sessions four days per week, with a dedicated computer-literacy session on Thursdays. This is a proposal, not a confirmed timetable, and may change after practical arrangements and learner needs are reviewed.

How progress could be reviewed

  • Can the learner complete a practical task with less support over time?
  • Can the learner apply a skill in a familiar everyday situation?
  • Which lessons or instructions need to be made clearer?
  • What feedback do learners share about the experience?

Progress should support learning, not rank or embarrass learners. Public impact claims will only be added when supported by real, appropriately collected evidence.

Privacy

Privacy by design.

This website is a front-end demonstration. It does not include a server-side account system or send form submissions to a database by default. Content edits and demo progress may be stored in the current browser's local storage.

Before using the site with real learners, add a complete privacy notice, confirm applicable school and legal requirements, configure secure authentication and database access, and document how personal information is collected, used, retained, and deleted.

Do not enter confidential, sensitive, or identifying learner information into this demo.

Accessibility

Designed to be approachable.

The interface aims to support keyboard navigation, readable text, responsive layouts, visible focus states, and reduced motion preferences. A full accessibility review with assistive technologies and representative users is still needed before claiming conformance to an accessibility standard.
